Technical Deep-Dive & Viva Prep

Everything you need to know about the mathematics, models, and design decisions behind StockVision AI.

Linear Regression assumes a rigid linear relationship between time and price ($y = mx + c$), which fails to capture market volatility and momentum reversals. LSTMs require heavy deep learning libraries (>150MB) and multi-second training times that violate serverless limits. Random Forest combines non-linear feature splits on technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages) with sub-50ms inference, making it lightweight and accurate for serverless web applications.

We employ an auto-regressive recursive strategy. The model first predicts Day $t+1$ using historical features. That predicted price is then appended to the simulated time series to recalculate rolling moving averages, dynamic RSI, and lag values to predict Day $t+2$. This process repeats for 30 iterations to extract 1-Day, 7-Day, and 30-Day price targets.

RSI computes momentum on a scale of 0 to 100 based on the ratio of 14-day average gains to average losses:
RSI = 100 - (100 / (1 + (Avg Gain / Avg Loss)))
An RSI below 30 signals an oversold condition (potential buying rebound), while an RSI above 70 indicates an overbought condition (potential pullback).

The backend fetches recent headlines via Yahoo Finance and normalizes the text into word tokens. It scores them against curated financial lexicons of Bullish words (surge, rally, profit, upgrade, record) and Bearish words (drop, plunge, miss, loss, lawsuit, crash) to compute a normalized sentiment score index from -100 to +100.
